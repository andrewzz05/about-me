About me

Why I build

I'm a computer science student at the University of Michigan with a particular interest in backend engineering, distributed systems, and applied machine learning.

I like technical problems that require more than simply making something work once. I'm interested in how software behaves under load, how systems recover from failure, and how thoughtful abstractions can make complicated systems easier to maintain.

My experience has ranged from compiler construction and distributed systems coursework to production software engineering internships and applied machine-learning projects. That range has made me comfortable moving between lower-level systems concepts and higher-level product requirements.

What I'm exploring

I'm especially interested in the intersection of backend infrastructure and intelligent software. I enjoy learning unfamiliar technologies, understanding the underlying systems, and then using that understanding to build something useful.
