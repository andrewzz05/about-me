Distributed Systems

A sequence of Go-based projects exploring how distributed services maintain useful behavior in the presence of concurrency, replication, failures, and changing system membership.

GoRPCReplicationFault tolerance

Concepts explored

Replication

Worked with replicated services and the coordination required to keep multiple nodes consistent.

Consensus

Studied how distributed nodes reach agreement despite unreliable communication and failures.

Sharding

Explored partitioning data across groups of servers and the coordination required when responsibility changes.

Failure handling

Designed around crashes, unavailable peers, retries, and other conditions that are normal in distributed environments.

What I learned

These projects changed how I think about backend engineering. A service can be locally correct while still behaving incorrectly as part of a larger system. Designing around concurrency, partial failure, and coordination requires making those assumptions explicit.

“Reliable systems are designed around failure, not just around the happy path.”
